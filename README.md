**VELAMAR 3D** is a sun-soaked island crime empire built with Three.js — a fully procedural tropical city rendered in the browser with zero network requests. Steal cars, outrun the law, build your crew, and take over five districts one job at a time.

## The island

Velamar is a living open-world city split into five districts, each with its own look, traffic, and heat: the working-class **Port**, the winding streets of the **Old Town**, the neon-lit **Beachfront**, the glass towers of the **Financial** district, and the yacht-lined **Marina**. Golden-hour dusk melts into a full night cycle as you play.

## Story jobs

Ten story jobs carry you from your first car theft to a five-star, cross-island escape. Missions chain together — each one raises the stakes, the police response, and the payout.

## Wanted by the VCPD

Every crime raises your wanted level, and the Velamar City Police Department answers in kind:

- **PIT maneuvers** — patrol cars ram your rear quarter to spin you out
- **Spike strips** — shred your tires if you charge through a trap
- **Roadblocks** — the streets ahead close off as the heat rises
- **SWAT van** — heavy response at the top wanted levels
- **HELIX-1 helicopter** — air support tracks you from above when the stars max out

Lose the cops, lay low, or call in your hacker to burn stars off your record.

## Steal anything with wheels

Sedan, sport car, van, or bike — if it drives, you can steal it and drive it. Every vehicle handles differently, and every theft adds heat. The boats in the marina are scenery only; the action stays on the streets.

## Combat

A four-tier weapon wheel and drive-by shooting let you fight from behind the wheel or on foot. Buy better hardware as your empire grows.

## Your crew

- **RICO** — your getaway driver, one call away when you need wheels fast
- **NOVA** — your hacker, who burns wanted stars off your record

Build territory standing district by district to lock in your control of the island.

## Side hustles

- **Street race league** — prove you're the fastest driver on Velamar
- **Taxi fares** — earn clean cash ferrying passengers around the city
- **Contraband crates** — risky pickups, bigger payouts
- **Stunt jumps** — find the ramps, catch air, earn respect

## Garage

Tune your rides with engine, armor, and nitro upgrades, plus fresh paint. Your garage is where stolen cars become getaway machines.

## Also in the game

- 3 save slots
- Live minimap
- WebAudio synth radio with 3 stations
- Golden-hour dusk and full night cycle

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080
